**[181]** This time you must beat Janie. The royal endorsement can only belong to Black Reef Bay.

这次你必须打败珍妮。皇家背书只能属于黑礁湾。

**[182]** Veil, maybe we should forget it this time.

薇尔，也许这次我们就算了吧。

**[183]** You know I haven't drawn a complete design in years.

你知道我已经好几年没画出完整的设计了。

**[184]** Lilith, I know you can do it. Once we win the contest, we'll get married right away.

莉莉丝，我知道你能行。等我们赢了比赛，我们就立刻结婚。

**[185]** Okay. All right. I'll do my best. [laughter] That's more like it.

好。好吧。我会尽力的。【笑声】这才像话。

**[186]** What should I do? My famous work was just one of Janie's discarded drafts.

我该怎么办？我那幅成名作，不过是珍妮丢弃的草稿之一。

**[187]** With my skills, I can't win this contest.

凭我这点本事，根本赢不了这场比赛。

**[188]** Madam, the lady of White Cliff Harbor has woken up.

夫人，白崖港的女主人醒了。

**[189]** Perfect timing. It seems heaven is helping me too.

来得正好。看来老天也在帮我。

**[190]** The competition is in a couple of days. If Blackberg Bay wins, Finn and Whiteclft Harbor will be in an even tougher spot.

比赛还有几天就到了。要是黑礁湾赢了，芬恩和白崖港的处境会更艰难。

**[191]** Mommy, you're not pretty when you frown.

妈咪，你皱眉的样子一点都不好看。

**[192]** Coral will help you. Wow, it's so pretty.

珊瑚来帮你。哇，好漂亮。

**[193]** Can Coral draw one with seagull's feather?

珊瑚能画一幅用海鸥羽毛的吗？

**[194]** Of course. You inspired this design. Shall I teach you to write your name?

当然可以。这个设计是你给我的灵感。要不要我教你写自己的名字？

**[195]** Yes. Huh? Why can't I see it? Silly girl.

好啊。咦？我怎么看不见？傻丫头。

**[196]** We didn't dip it in ink. Danny, I heard you woke up, so I came to see you.

我们没蘸墨水。珍妮，听说你醒了，我就过来看看你。

**[197]** Bad woman. This child again. She keeps ruining my plans.

坏女人。又是这孩子。她总坏我的事。

**[198]** What bad luck. Coral, what did you just say?

真倒霉。珊瑚，你刚才说什么？

**[199]** [gasps] She's just playing around. Coral, go help mommy check if my medicine is ready.

【倒吸一口气】她只是在闹着玩。珊瑚，去帮妈咪看看药好了没有。

**[200]** Okay, Janie, I was so worried while you were in a coma.

好。珍妮，你昏迷的时候我真的好担心。

**[201]** We used to be good friends. I was in a coma for 5 years, and Miss Lilith never visited me once.

我们以前可是好朋友。我昏迷了五年，莉莉丝小姐却一次都没来看过我。

**[202]** Leave. I need to take my medicine. Janie, you forced me to do this.

出去。我要吃药了。珍妮，是你逼我走到这一步的。

**[203]** Bad woman. You stole mommy's drawing. [snorts] [laughter] Give it back.

坏女人。你偷了妈咪的画。【冷哼】【笑声】还给我。

**[204]** That's mommy's drawing. You little jinx.

那是妈咪的画。你这个小扫把星。

**[205]** Ruining my plans again. Since you came to me, don't blame me for being cruel.

又坏我的事。既然你自己送上门来，就别怪我心狠。

**[206]** CAROL. FATHER CAROL is missing. Hurry and send men to search for her.

珊瑚！快，珊瑚失踪了。赶紧派人去找她。

**[207]** [laughter] Seal the docks. Question every sailor.

【笑声】封锁码头。盘问每一个水手。

**[208]** Let me go. Blame yourself for being born wrong and getting in my way.

放开我。怪就怪你生错了，还碍我的事。

**[209]** This is Miss Coral's broo. Below are undercurrents and sharp reefs.

这是珊瑚小姐的船。下面是暗流和尖礁。

**[210]** Could she already be? Sure. Don't be afraid.

她该不会已经……？大人，别害怕。

**[211]** Up. Up. Your grace. There's movement on the water.

上面！上面！公爵大人，水面上有动静。

**[212]** So, Coral really has special powers. Hurry, save my daughter.

这么说，珊瑚真的有特殊能力。快，救我女儿。

**[213]** We can't let anyone know about Coral's powers or people will use her as a tool.

绝不能让人知道珊瑚的能力，否则会被人当工具利用。

**[214]** Coral is still unconscious and the design is gone.

珊瑚还昏迷着，设计图也不见了。

**[215]** What do we do about tomorrow's contest? Don't worry.

明天的比赛怎么办？别担心。

**[216]** I'll find a way. Juke Finn, I heard White Cliff Harbor didn't submit a design.

我会想办法的。芬恩公爵，听说白崖港没有提交设计。

**[217]** I guess your wife has run out of talent and can't make anything decent.

看来你夫人才思已尽，做不出像样的东西了。

**[218]** With Lilith's skills, she'll only come last.

凭莉莉丝的本事，她只能垫底。

**[219]** Don't celebrate too soon. Lila's work will win first place.

别高兴得太早。莉莉丝的作品会拿第一。

**[220]** Today I will be the winner. Next we present Miss Lilith's work.

今天的赢家会是我。接下来展示莉莉丝小姐的作品。

**[221]** That is my design. That's my stolen draft.

那是我的设计。那是我被偷走的草稿。

**[222]** Lilith, it really was you. If you say I stole it, where is your proof?

莉莉丝，果然是你。你说我偷的，证据呢？

**[223]** Oh no. [gasps and laughter] Lilith was the only one there.

完了。【倒吸一口气，笑声】当时只有莉莉丝在场。

**[224]** If you have no proof, kneel and apologize to Lilith.

没有证据，就跪下向莉莉丝道歉。

**[225]** Coral, wake up. YOUR MOM AND DAD [screaming] ARE being bullied.

珊瑚，快醒醒。你的爸爸妈妈【尖叫】正在被人欺负。
