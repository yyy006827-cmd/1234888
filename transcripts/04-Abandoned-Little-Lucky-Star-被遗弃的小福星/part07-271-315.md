**[271]** Sir, the bird isn't dead. I can save it.

露米醒了。露米真的活过来了。露米说你不能再喂它那么多，不然肚子会撑破。

**[273]** Okay. Okay. As long as Lumi lives, I'll listen to whatever you say.

好。好。只要露米活着，你说什么我都听。

**[274]** Here, wear this. What is this? It feels cool and so nice.

来，戴上这个。这是什么？凉凉的，好舒服。

**[275]** This is a royal heirloom. You saved my Lumi.

这是皇室传家宝。你救了我的露米。

**[276]** I'll give it to you as a gift. Your Majesty, why is Coral with you?

我把它送给你。陛下，珊瑚怎么会跟您在一起？

**[277]** Is this your daughter? She got lost and I happened to run into her in the garden.

这是您的女儿吗？她迷路了，我碰巧在花园里遇见她。

**[278]** Duke Finn's young lady is wearing the royal ancestral gem.

芬恩公爵家的小姐戴着皇室祖传宝石。

**[279]** Your Majesty, this is too precious. Please take it back.

陛下，这太贵重了。请收回吧。

**[280]** What if Coral accidentally damages it? It's fine.

要是珊瑚不小心弄坏了怎么办？没关系。

**[281]** I want to make Coral my godaughter. Do you agree?

我想认珊瑚做干女儿。你同意吗？

**[282]** This is Coral's honor, but please allow me to ask her opinion.

这是珊瑚的荣幸，但请允许我问问她的意见。

**[283]** Coral, are you willing? I am. [laughter] Then it's settled.

珊瑚，你愿意吗？我愿意。【笑声】那就这么定了。

**[284]** Wait. Coral is my biological daughter. [screaming] You are not my daddy.

等等。珊瑚是我的亲生女儿。【尖叫】你才不是我爸爸。

**[285]** You've done so many terrible things. How could you be Coral's real father?

你做了那么多坏事。你怎么可能是珊瑚的亲爸爸？

**[286]** The birthmark, hair, and birth records all match.

胎记、头发和出生记录全都对得上。

**[287]** I am your real father. Finn only took my daughter to please the royal family.

我才是你的亲爸爸。芬恩只不过是为了讨好皇室才带走我女儿的。

**[288]** How is that possible? Juke Finn, how many benefits have you gained through my daughter?

怎么可能？芬恩公爵，你借着我女儿捞了多少好处？

**[289]** You schemed with bad intentions. It's time to stop.

你心怀不轨、处心积虑。该收手了。

**[290]** Juke Val, I'm not scum like you. Coral may not be my biological daughter, but I've always loved her as my own.

薇尔公爵，我不像你这种人渣。珊瑚或许不是我的亲生女儿，但我一直视她如己出。

**[291]** Coral really is your biological daughter?

珊瑚真的是你的亲生女儿？

**[292]** Absolutely. Coral is my biological daughter.

当然。珊瑚是我的亲生女儿。

**[293]** Your Majesty, you said you would make Coral your godaughter.

陛下，您说过要认珊瑚做干女儿。

**[294]** That's no problem. But Black Reef Way has had some trouble lately, and we'd want the royal family to help.

这没问题。不过黑礁湾最近出了些麻烦，希望皇室能帮忙。

**[295]** How bold. You dare make demands of the royal family.

好大的胆子。你竟敢向皇室提条件。

**[296]** You are not my daddy. His majesty won't help a bad person like you.

你才不是我爸爸。陛下不会帮你这种坏人。

**[297]** Coral, is that how you talk to me? I'm your real father.

珊瑚，你就这么跟我说话？我才是你的亲爸爸。

**[298]** You're the one who abandoned Coral back then.

当年抛弃珊瑚的人就是你。

**[299]** How does he know I abandoned Coral back then?

他怎么知道我当年抛弃了珊瑚？

**[300]** Impossible. He must be bluffing. Don't talk nonsense.

不可能。他一定在虚张声势。别胡说八道。

**[301]** Abandoned. That's absurd. Then tell me, how did Coral leave Black Reef Bay back then?

抛弃？荒谬。那你说说，当年珊瑚是怎么离开黑礁湾的？

**[302]** Coral got lost when she was very young. I searched for her for a long time, but couldn't find her?

珊瑚很小的时候就走丢了。我找了很久，都没找到她。

**[303]** Do you really think I couldn't find out what happened 5 years ago?

你真以为五年前的事我查不出来吗？

**[304]** Butler, bring in the witness. It's her. This is the midwife who delivered Lady Veil's baby back then.

管家，把证人带上来。是她。这就是当年给薇尔夫人生孩子的接生婆。

**[305]** Please tell us what happened. Duke Vile believed the fortune tellers claimed that Miss Coral was a jinx and personally threw her into the sea.

请说说当时发生了什么。薇尔公爵听信算命人说珊瑚小姐是灾星，亲自把她扔进了海里。

**[306]** The lady also passed away from a broken heart.

夫人也因伤心过度去世了。

**[307]** She's lying. Juke Finn paid off the midwife to frame me.

她在说谎。芬恩公爵收买接生婆来陷害我。

**[308]** Juke Veil, I'm not trying to frame you. I'm only protecting my family.

薇尔公爵，我不是要陷害你。我只是在保护我的家人。

**[309]** Coral is my biological daughter. NO ONE CAN CHANGE BLOOD [screaming] FLIES.

珊瑚是我的亲生女儿。血缘关系谁也【尖叫】改变不了。

**[310]** VEIL, you are charged with child abandonment, murder, abduction, and smuggling.

薇尔，你被指控弃婴、谋杀、绑架和走私。

**[311]** You will be held in prison pending sentencing.

你将被关押候审，等待宣判。

**[312]** Take him away. I won't let this rest. I won't let this rest.

把他带走。我不会善罢甘休。我不会善罢甘休。

**[313]** [laughter] [snorts] Brother, did I act well?

【笑声】【哼声】哥哥，我演得好吗？

**[314]** Very well, Coral. The nautical academy will start soon.

演得很好，珊瑚。航海学院马上就要开学了。

你想去上学吗？学校是什么？
