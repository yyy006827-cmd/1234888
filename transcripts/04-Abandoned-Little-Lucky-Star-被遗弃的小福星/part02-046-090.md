**[046]** Daddy's bringing food to mom and brother.

爸爸要给妈妈和哥哥送吃的。

**[047]** Why don't mom and brother come too? Your mother Janie was caught in a shipwreck and has rested in slumber for many years.

为什么妈妈和哥哥不一起来？你母亲珍妮遭遇海难，已经沉睡很多年了。

**[048]** Your brother Mark injured his leg in an accident and refuses to see anyone.

你哥哥马克在一次意外中伤了腿，谁也不见。

**[049]** Daddy Coral wants to come, too. Okay. Janie, this is our child, Coral.

爸爸，珊瑚也想去。好。珍妮，这是我们的孩子，珊瑚。

**[050]** You don't know how lovely she is. Like a gift from the sea.

你不知道她有多可爱。像大海送来的礼物。

**[051]** Mommy is so pretty. Like a mermaid sleeping in a shell.

妈妈好漂亮。像睡在贝壳里的美人鱼。

**[052]** Mommy, wake up. Coral and daddy are waiting for you.

妈妈，醒醒。珊瑚和爸爸都在等你。

**[053]** She can definitely hear you. Coral, wait here for Daddy.

她一定听得见你。珊瑚，在这儿等爸爸。

**[054]** Your brother has a bad temper, and I'm afraid he'll scare you.

你哥哥脾气很坏，我怕他吓到你。

**[055]** Coral wants to see, brother. Coral isn't scared.

珊瑚想看哥哥。珊瑚不怕。

**[056]** Mark, I brought you dinner. I'm not easy.

马克，我给你带了晚饭。我不饿。

**[057]** Leave me alone. Brother sounds very sad because he has suffered too much.

别管我。哥哥听起来好难过，因为他吃了太多苦。

**[058]** [snorts] Brother is in danger. I'm only a burden.

【冷哼】哥哥有危险。我只是个累赘。

**[059]** It's better if I disappear. Brother D over there.

我还是消失比较好。哥哥，快看那边。

**[060]** The waves are about to swallow the boat.

浪要把船吞掉了。

**[061]** [screaming] Brother, hold on to me. You're not useless.

【尖叫】哥哥，抓紧我。你不是没用的人。

**[062]** You're my brother. [laughter] Mark, try one more time.

你是我哥哥。【笑声】马克，再试一次。

**[063]** A new doctor has come to the capital. Maybe he can help.

京城来了位新医生。也许他能帮上忙。

**[064]** If I could be healed, I would have been long ago.

要是能治好，我早就好了。

**[065]** You ruined everything. What do you want, Mark?

你毁了一切。马克，你想干什么？

**[066]** Coral saved your life. If she hadn't arrived in time, you would have Brother, I can heal you.

珊瑚救了你的命。要不是她及时赶到，你早就——哥哥，我能治好你。

**[067]** Little liar. I'm not your brother. Get out.

小骗子。我不是你哥哥。滚出去。

**[068]** All of you. Get out. Don't you understand?

你们全都滚出去。听不懂吗？

**[069]** If you don't leave, don't blame me for being rough.

再不走，就别怪我对你们不客气了。

**[070]** [gasps] What? What are you doing? Don't touch me.

【倒吸一口气】什么？你在干什么？别碰我。

**[071]** I'm helping you. I'm not a little liar. I can heal your legs.

我在帮你。我不是小骗子。我能治好你的腿。

**[072]** My legs. I can feel them. Brother is grown up now.

我的腿。我有感觉了。哥哥已经长大了。

**[073]** You have to learn to walk by yourself. This must be a coincidence.

你得自己学会走路。这一定是巧合。

**[074]** You You should go out. I need to rest. Then let's Pinky promise.

你……你出去吧。我要休息。那我们拉钩约定。

**[075]** Don't do anything this dangerous again. Okay, Grandpa.

以后不许再做这么危险的事了。好，爷爷。

**[076]** That's too much. Grandpa, I want to see the new doctor.

太过分了。爷爷，我想去见那位新医生。

**[077]** Good, Mark. Whatever else you want, just tell me.

好，马克。还有什么想要的，尽管跟我说。

**[078]** It's all thanks to Coral. No, it's brother's own resolve that matters most.

这都多亏了珊瑚。不，最重要的是哥哥自己的决心。

**[079]** Ever since she came, good things have happened one after another.

自从她来了，好事一件接一件。

**[080]** God has blessed us. Feels privat took two trade routes.

上帝保佑我们。对手抢走了两条贸易航线。

**[081]** White Cliffport's funds are cut off. Father, I plan to bid on the sunken cargo containers.

白崖港的资金被切断了。父亲，我打算竞拍那些沉没的货柜。

**[082]** Isn't that too risky? Those unknown containers could be booby trapped.

那不是太冒险了吗？那些来历不明的货柜说不定有陷阱。

**[083]** Right now, only the sunken treasure can save White Crypt Port.

眼下，只有沉没的宝藏才能救白崖港。

**[084]** Daddy looks unhappy. I have to help him.

爸爸看起来不开心。我得帮帮他。

**[085]** Daddy, can I go with you? Of course. I'll bring my little lucky star.

爸爸，我能跟你一起去吗？当然。我要带上我的小福星。

**[086]** Daddy, are these boxes sleeping under the sea?

爸爸，这些箱子是在海底睡觉吗？

**[087]** Yes. Pick one you like and daddy will buy it for you.

是啊。挑一个你喜欢的，爸爸买给你。

**[088]** I don't know why, but having Coral here makes me feel at ease.

也不知道为什么，有珊瑚在身边，我就安心多了。

**[089]** Duke Finn, you were lucky the shipwreck didn't kill you.

芬恩公爵，你上次船难没死，算你走运。

**[090]** This time, I'll make you lose everything.

这一次，我要让你失去一切。
