**[001]** Don't throw my child away. The moment she was born, my ship sank, just as the fortune Saver predicted.

别扔掉我的孩子。她一出生，我的船就沉了，和算命先生说的一模一样。

**[002]** She's indeed a curse. Back off. No. [screaming] A human child.

她果然是个祸害。退后。不。【尖叫】一个人类的孩子。

**[003]** She can breathe underwater. She has a special energy about her.

她能在水下呼吸。身上有种特别的能量。

**[004]** She's actually brought the ocean back to life.

她居然让整片海都恢复了生机。

**[005]** [panting] Don't worry, Mommy. I'll heal daddy.

【喘息】别担心，妈妈。我会治好爸爸的。

**[006]** Thank you, my sweet coral. It's been 5 years since you came to us, and every creature in the sea sees you as a true goddess.

谢谢你，我的小珊瑚。你来到我们身边已经五年了，海里的每一种生物都把你当成真正的女神。

**[007]** The coral is turning white and the currents are wrong.

珊瑚在变白，洋流也不对劲了。

**[008]** Many little fish have to move to other waters.

好多小鱼都得搬去别的水域了。

**[009]** Coral, it's time to find your family. On shore, there are others like you who walk on two legs.

珊瑚，是时候去找你的家人了。岸上有和你一样用两条腿走路的人。

**[010]** Go. My family. Wow. Is this the nest on land?

去吧。我的家人。哇。这就是陆地上的巢穴吗？

**[011]** It's so tall. Just be careful, little one.

好高啊。小心点，小家伙。

**[012]** Thank you, Granny. One gold pound. Coral, come with me.

谢谢您，奶奶。一金磅。珊瑚，跟我来。

**[013]** I saw your human father. He's at the dock.

我看见你的人类父亲了。他在码头。

**[014]** I finally got to meet Daddy. Vale, I'm having a boy.

我终于见到爸爸了。薇尔，我怀了个男孩。

**[015]** When can we have our wedding? Don't rush.

我们什么时候能办婚礼？别急。

**[016]** Once I deal with Duke Finn, I promise I'll throw you the most lavish wedding in the entire capital.

等我解决了芬恩公爵，我保证给你办一场全京城最奢华的婚礼。

**[017]** Coral, that's him. Your father, Daddy, I found you.

珊瑚，就是他。你的父亲。爸爸，我找到你了。

**[018]** Where did this wild kid come from? The seagull told me you're my father.

这野孩子哪来的？海鸥告诉我，你是我爸爸。

**[019]** It can't be wrong. You of fish. Disgusting.

不会错的。你这鱼人。恶心。

**[020]** Get away. She's the curse. I'm just overthinking.

滚开。她就是那个祸害。我只是想多了。

**[021]** That child can't still be alive. Seagull.

那孩子不可能还活着。海鸥。

**[022]** Doesn't my human father like me? [screaming] Look, someone's tampered with the helm.

我的人类爸爸不喜欢我吗？【尖叫】快看，有人动过舵了。

**[023]** Coral, that ship is about to break apart.

珊瑚，那艘船快要散架了。

**[024]** [screaming] Go. See, be still. There are people on the ship.

【尖叫】去吧。海啊，平静下来。船上还有人。

**[025]** This This is a miracle from God. [gasps] There's a CHILD IN THE SEA.

这……这简直是上帝的奇迹。【倒吸一口气】海里有个孩子！

**[026]** LOWER THE [screaming] ROPE. Little one, how did you fall into the sea?

放下【尖叫】绳子！小家伙，你怎么掉进海里的？

**[027]** Are you hurt? I'll take you to see a doctor.

你受伤了吗？我带你去看医生。

**[028]** I'm all right, sir. Don't worry. Where is your family?

我没事，先生。别担心。你的家人呢？

**[029]** Daddy doesn't want me. I'm all alone now.

爸爸不要我了。我现在一个人了。

**[030]** Then let me be your daddy. Okay. I have a daddy now.

那就让我当你的爸爸吧。好。我有爸爸了。

**[031]** Yes. Daddy will take you home. What a big, beautiful stone cave.

嗯。爸爸带你回家。好大好漂亮的石洞啊。

**[032]** [gasps] This is a home, not a stone cave.

【倒吸一口气】这是家，不是石洞。

**[033]** Juke, the old juke suddenly worsened. The doctor says he may not last the night.

公爵，老公爵突然恶化了。医生说他可能撑不过今晚。

**[034]** [snorts] I've done all I can. His pulse is almost gone.

【冷哼】我已经尽力了。他的脉搏几乎摸不到了。

**[035]** Father. Father, wake up. Grandpa's waking up.

父亲。父亲，醒醒。爷爷醒了。

**[036]** Don't try to comfort me. [sighs] [snorts] Finn, who is this little girl?

别安慰我了。【叹气】【冷哼】芬恩，这个小姑娘是谁？

**[037]** Strange. My body feels much lighter. This is a medical miracle.

奇怪。我的身体轻多了。这简直是医学奇迹。

**[038]** An incurable illness just suddenly healed.

治不好的病突然就好了。

**[039]** I chase the bad things out of Grandpa's body.

我把爷爷身体里的坏东西赶出去了。

**[040]** Father, her name is Coral. She is my adopted daughter.

父亲，她叫珊瑚。是我的养女。

**[041]** Hello, Grandpa. Such a good girl. You make me feel alive.

爷爷好。真是个好孩子。你让我觉得又活过来了。

**[042]** You're our little lucky star. Father's been sick for half an year, but he's feeling better barely a day after Coral arrived.

你是我们的小福星。父亲病了半年，珊瑚才来一天他就好多了。

**[043]** Could she really be our lucky star? Fetch some hot soup at once.

她真的是我们的小福星吗？赶紧去盛点热汤来。

**[044]** We can't let our little lucky star go hungry.

可不能让我们的小福星饿着。

**[045]** We eat with spoons. [snorts] Coral, eat first.

我们用勺子吃饭。【冷哼】珊瑚，先吃。
