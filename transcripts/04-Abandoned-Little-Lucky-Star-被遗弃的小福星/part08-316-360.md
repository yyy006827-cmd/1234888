**[316]** You can learn seaw roots, stars, and many fun things.

你可以学航线、星象，还有很多有趣的东西。

**[317]** I can go with you. [sighs and gasps] Yes, Coral wants to go to school with brother.

我可以跟你一起去。【叹息与喘息】好，珊瑚要跟哥哥一起去上学。

**[318]** [laughter] Good. Coral, [screaming] the children should be home by now.

【笑声】好。珊瑚，【尖叫】孩子们这会儿该回家了。

**[319]** Why aren't they back yet? Could something have happened?

他们怎么还没回来？会不会出什么事了？

**[320]** Don't scare yourself. Maybe they're playing together and lost track of time.

别自己吓自己。也许他们在一起玩，忘了时间。

**[321]** [gasps] The port guard said the children were taken to an old smuggling ship in Black Reef Bay.

【倒吸一口气】港口守卫说孩子们被带到黑礁湾一艘旧走私船上了。

**[322]** Bring 1 million gold pounds or both children will be buried under the sea.

带一百万金镑来，否则两个孩子都会葬身海底。

**[323]** Prepare a ship. We're going to Black Reef Bay.

准备一艘船。我们去黑礁湾。

**[324]** Little crabs, please help me untie the ropes.

小螃蟹们，请帮我解开绳子。

**[325]** Sister, can you really communicate with animals?

姐姐，你真的能跟动物说话吗？

**[326]** Yes, but they can only help. They can't testify for us.

能，但它们只能帮忙。没法替我们作证。

**[327]** Where did this filthy thing come from? Get lost.

哪来的脏东西？滚开。

**[328]** Little curse. Who are you talking to just now?

小灾星。你刚才在跟谁说话？

**[329]** You villain for tying my brother and me.

你这个坏蛋，竟然把我和弟弟绑起来。

**[330]** You will den get what you deserved. [laughter] You still dare talk back?

你会得到报应的。【笑声】你还敢顶嘴？

**[331]** Let Coral go. Neither of you is getting away.

放了珊瑚。你们谁也逃不掉。

**[332]** Veil. Coral. Mark. Dad. [screaming] Give me back the children.

薇尔。珊瑚。马克。爸爸。【尖叫】把孩子们还给我。

**[333]** I'll give you whatever you want. 1 million gold pounds and a ship to take us out of the country.

你要什么我都给你。一百万金镑，再给一艘船送我们离开这个国家。

**[334]** And you kneel. Fine. Don't do anything rash.

还有，你跪下。好。别轻举妄动。

**[335]** I'll kneel. Just don't hurt them. Daddy, don't kneel.

我跪。只要别伤害他们。爸爸，别跪。

**[336]** Stupid birds. My eyes. Don't be scared. [screaming] EVERYONE STAYS RIGHT THERE.

该死的鸟。我的眼睛。别怕。【尖叫】全都给我站住。

**[337]** MOVE AGAIN AND I'LL KILL HER. VALE, DON'T DO anything crazy.

再动我就杀了她。薇尔，别做傻事。

**[338]** IT'S ALL HER FAULT. I FINALLY BUILT THE LIFE I have today.

都是她害的。我好不容易才有了今天的日子。

**[339]** IF IT WEREN'T for her, I wouldn't have been caught.

要不是因为她，我也不会被抓。

**[340]** Coral didn't hurt you. You did bad things.

珊瑚没有害你。是你自己做了坏事。

**[341]** SHUT UP. TODAY, NO ONE HERE GETS OUT ALIVE.

闭嘴。今天在场的人一个都别想活着出去。

**[342]** DAD. [screaming] CARL. [gasps] It's too [laughter] late.

爸爸。【尖叫】马克。【倒吸一口气】已经太【笑声】晚了。

**[343]** Uncle Whale, come help us. The ship is sinking.

鲸叔叔，快来帮我们。船要沉了。

**[344]** Dad, brother, jump into the sea quickly.

爸爸，弟弟，快跳进海里。

**[345]** Don't be afraid. [laughter] IT'S SO FAR FROM SHORE HERE.

别害怕。【笑声】这儿离岸边可远了。

**[346]** Let's see how long you can hold on. Sister sharks, where are you?

看你们还能撑多久。鲨鱼姐姐们，你们在哪儿？

**[347]** [screaming] Why do all these animals obey coral?

【尖叫】为什么这些动物都听珊瑚的？

**[348]** If I can't have Coral, THEN NO ONE CAN. ALL OF YOU DIE.

我得不到珊瑚，那就谁也别想得到。你们全都去死。

**[349]** I won't let anyone die. Coral. Carl, her body is too weak.

我不会让任何人死的。珊瑚。马克，她的身体太虚弱了。

**[350]** She may not wake up. No, my child won't leave us.

她可能醒不过来了。不，我的孩子不会离开我们。

**[351]** Coral, Dad is here. You said you would protect us.

珊瑚，爸爸在这儿。你说过会保护我们。

**[352]** Now it's our turn to protect you. Please wake up.

现在轮到我们保护你了。求求你醒过来。

**[353]** Okay, [laughter] sister, I still have to go to the nautical academy with you.

好，【笑声】姐姐，我还要跟你一起去航海学院呢。

**[354]** Carl, can you hear dad? Dad. Mom. [gasps and laughter] Mom is here.

马克，能听见爸爸说话吗？爸爸。妈妈。【倒吸一口气与笑声】妈妈来了。

**[355]** She's awake. My sister is awake. As long as you're awake, that's all that matters.

她醒了。姐姐醒了。只要你醒着，其他什么都不重要。

**[356]** Did Coral sleep for a long time? Not long.

珊瑚睡了很久吗？不久。

**[357]** As long as you wake up, no time is too long.

只要你醒来，再久都不算久。

**[358]** [laughter] Coral is now officially the legally adopted daughter of Duke Finn of White Cliff Harbor and is under royal protection.

【笑声】珊瑚现已正式成为白崖港芬恩公爵的合法养女，并受到皇室保护。

**[359]** From now on, the palace is your home, too.

从今往后，王宫也是你的家。

**[360]** White Cliff Harbor will protect her, too.

白崖港也会保护她。
