**[091]** Coral, I can see inside. I'll help you choose the best treasure crate.

珊瑚，我能看到里面。我帮你挑最好的宝箱。

**[092]** Thank you, little crab. Coral, pick this one.

谢谢你，小螃蟹。珊瑚，就选这个。

**[093]** Okay, this one is for daddy and that one is for mommy.

好，这个给爸爸，那个给妈妈。

**[094]** It's you, little liar. Nice imagination.

又是你，小骗子。想象力倒挺丰富。

**[095]** Those beat up crates are just garbage. H the little crab said this box has something good inside.

那些破箱子就是垃圾。嘿，小螃蟹说这个箱子里有好东西。

**[096]** Send the boxes coral just named to my home.

把珊瑚点名的箱子送到我家去。

**[097]** Yes, your grace. Juke Finn White Cliff Herber is falling apart and you still waste funds spoiling your adopted daughter.

是，公爵大人。芬恩公爵，白崖港都快塌了，你还浪费钱娇惯你的养女。

**[098]** I hear you're begging other jukes for money lately.

我听说你最近在向其他公爵求援借钱。

**[099]** I'll make sure Finn never walks out of this auction hall.

我会让芬恩永远走不出这座拍卖厅。

**[100]** Welcome all to the treasures final auction. 10 sunken crates are open for free bidding.

欢迎各位来到珍宝最终拍卖。十个沉船木箱开放自由竞拍。

**[101]** The one whose crate folds the highest value items gains the Royal Trade Partnership.

谁的箱子里装的东西估值最高，谁就能获得皇家贸易合作权。

**[102]** This is a huge business deal. If someone gets the royal endorsement through this, they'll rise fast in the capital.

这可是一笔大生意。谁要是靠这个拿到皇家背书，在首都会迅速崛起。

**[103]** Then let the bidding begin. Daddy, it's starting. 20,000 gold for chest number three.

那就开始出价吧。爸爸，开始了。三号箱，两万金币。

**[104]** That's way too much. Duke Veil seems sure he'll win. 20,000 once, 20,000 twice.

也太贵了。薇尔公爵好像胜券在握。两万一次，两万两次。

**[105]** Who would dare bid against that? 20,000 sold.

谁敢跟他抢？两万成交。

**[106]** Congratulations, Duke Vale. Estimated value 200,000 gold pounds.

恭喜薇尔公爵。估值约二十万金镑。

**[107]** [laughter] Duke Finn, you cannot win this.

【笑声】芬恩公爵，你赢不了的。

**[108]** Your turn, Juke Finn. Coral, have you chosen?

轮到你了，芬恩公爵。珊瑚，你选好了吗？

**[109]** Number 10. Number 10. Chest 10 has blue tide stone like stars in the deep sea.

十号。十号。十号箱里有蓝潮石，像深海里的星星。

**[110]** Thank you, little crab. Daddy, I want that one.

谢谢你，小螃蟹。爸爸，我要那个。

**[111]** All right, I bid 50,000. Chest number 10.

好，我出价五万。十号箱。

**[112]** Spending 50,000 on rotten wood. Finn, has losing your pride driven you mad?

花五万买烂木头。芬恩，你是输到把体面都丢光，疯了吗？

**[113]** Daddy, trust me, we'll win. Daddy believes in coral.

爸爸，相信我，我们会赢的。爸爸相信珊瑚。

**[114]** I'm spending 50,000 to make my daughter happy?

我花五万，就是为了让女儿开心。

**[115]** My family can still afford that. Thank goodness.

我家还负担得起。谢天谢地。

**[116]** Spending 50,000 just to make a child happy.

花五万就为了哄一个孩子开心。

**[117]** White Cliff Harbor is finished. Finn, since you're so confident, talk is boring.

白崖港完了。芬恩，既然你这么有把握，空谈没意思。

**[118]** How about we raise the stakes and make the prince's auction more exciting?

不如加码，让王子的拍卖更刺激一点？

**[119]** What stakes do you want? We bet on the chests we bought.

你想加什么注？就拿我们买下的箱子来赌。

**[120]** If mine is worth more, you hand over half of White Cliff Harbor's trade routes.

如果我的更值钱，你把白崖港一半的贸易航线交出来。

**[121]** And if I win, if you win, all of Black Reef Bay's privateing profits this year are yours.

要是我赢了——如果你赢了，黑礁湾今年所有私掠利润都归你。

**[122]** I don't want your dirty money. If I win, you return everything you stole.

我不要你的脏钱。如果我赢了，你把抢来的东西全都还回来。

**[123]** Black Reef Bay becomes mine. and you kneel and apologize to me in public.

黑礁湾归我，你还得当众跪下向我道歉。

**[124]** I've already bribed the attendants to check these chests, except for mine.

我早就贿赂过侍从，查过这些箱子，除了我自己的。

**[125]** The rest are worthless trash. Since you want to die, I'll help you.

剩下的全是不值钱的垃圾。既然你想找死，我就成全你。

**[126]** Deal. Daddy, trust me. We'll win. All right.

成交。爸爸，相信我。我们会赢的。好。

**[127]** Daddy absolutely trust enough. Open the chest already.

爸爸绝对相信你。快开箱吧。

**[128]** I'll make you lose fair and square. Impossible.

我会让你输得心服口服。不可能。

**[129]** Looks like it was just sea salt reflecting light.

看来不过是海盐反光罢了。

**[130]** There's nothing inside. How foolish. A bet is a bet.

里面什么都没有。真蠢。赌约就是赌约。

**[131]** Sign it. From today on, White Cry Harbor is mine.

签字吧。从今天起，白崖港是我的了。

**[132]** No, the blue is still under the side of the box.

不对，蓝色还在箱子侧面下面。

**[133]** Pry it open from the edge again. Little girl, thanks to you, your father just lost his fortune.

再从边缘撬开。小姑娘，多亏了你，你父亲刚把家底输光了。

**[134]** Duke Finn, what a fine daughter you found.

芬恩公爵，你可真找了个好女儿。

**[135]** [laughter] She is my daughter. Anyone who insults her again will answer to me.

【笑声】她就是我女儿。谁再侮辱她，就跟我算账。
