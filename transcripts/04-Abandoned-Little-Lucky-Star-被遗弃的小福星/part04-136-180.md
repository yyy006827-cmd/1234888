**[136]** [snorts] Duke Finn, his royal highness, the prince, has arrived.

【哼声】芬恩公爵，王子殿下驾到。

**[137]** Your Highness, forgive me for not greeting you sooner.

殿下，恕我迎驾来迟。

我的箱子才是这里最值钱的。芬恩公爵输了却不肯认。

**[139]** Your Highness, there's something even better in my father's chest.

殿下，我父亲的箱子里还有更好的东西。

**[140]** Press here, then pry it open again. Why pry it again?

按这里，然后再撬开。为什么还要再撬？

**[141]** It's the blue stone where I used to hide for hide and seek.

就是那块蓝石头，我以前捉迷藏时躲过的地方。

**[142]** This is blue tide stone, a once- ina century treasure.

这是蓝潮石，百年一遇的珍宝。

**[143]** It's so amazing. The blue tide stone is valued at 1 million gold pounds.

太神奇了。这块蓝潮石估值一百万金镑。

**[144]** The royal family will buy it as the main stone for the coral jewelry contest.

王室将收购它，作为珊瑚珠宝大赛的主石。

**[145]** Impossible. Finn knows nothing. How could he open a chest with blue tide stone?

不可能。芬恩什么都不懂。他怎么可能打开装有蓝潮石的箱子？

**[146]** This must be fake. Are you questioning the royal appracial?

这一定是假的。你是在质疑皇家鉴定吗？

**[147]** Juke Veil, a bet is a bet. Return all the goods you stole.

薇尔公爵，赌约就是赌约。把你抢来的货全还回来。

**[148]** Black Reef Bay is mine. Fine. And kneel down and apologize to me.

黑礁湾是我的了。好。还得跪下向我道歉。

**[149]** Sorry, Finn. One day I will repay today's humiliation 100fold.

对不起，芬恩。总有一天，我会把今日的屈辱百倍奉还。

**[150]** Coral is amazing. You help daddy solve the family crisis.

珊瑚真棒。你帮爸爸化解了家族危机。

**[151]** Daddy will treat you to a feast. Daddy is the best.

爸爸请你大餐。爸爸最好了。

**[152]** It's all that brat's fault for ruining my plan.

都怪那个小鬼坏了我的计划。

**[153]** That is how does she have that birthark?

等等，她怎么会有那块胎记？

**[154]** If she really is that child, maybe she isn't a curse, but a seaborn lucky star who can change fate.

如果她真是那个孩子，或许她不是诅咒，而是能改写命运的人鱼小福星。

**[155]** A week later, the jewelry competition will feature the blue tried stone as the main gemstone.

一周后，珠宝大赛将以蓝潮石作为主宝石。

**[156]** The winning piece will be presented to the king at the birthday sea festival.

获胜作品将在生日海祭上呈献给国王。

**[157]** My chance is here. Lilith is a famous designer.

我的机会来了。莉莉丝是著名设计师。

**[158]** As long as we beat Finn, I'll take back today's humiliation with interest.

只要我们打败芬恩，今日的屈辱我连本带利讨回来。

**[159]** Dad, mom is the greatest designer. If she enters, she will ding win the competition.

爸爸，妈妈是最了不起的设计师。她要是参赛，一定能赢。

**[160]** Don't you remember? Mom is still in a coma and can't take part.

你忘了吗？妈妈还在昏迷，没法参赛。

**[161]** She will definitely wake up. Yo, Grace, Madam's finger moved.

她一定会醒的。公爵大人，夫人的手指动了。

**[162]** Janie is awake. When I was wiping Madam's hand, I saw her finger move.

珍妮醒了。我给夫人擦手时，看见她手指动了一下。

**[163]** That's a good sign. Don't worry, Daddy. Mommy will wake up soon.

这是好兆头。别担心，爸爸。妈妈很快就会醒。

**[164]** All right. We'll wait for her together. Sister Bird, how can I help mommy wake up sooner?

好。我们一起等她。小鸟姐姐，我怎样才能让妈妈快点醒来？

**[165]** The first drop of dew on the sea cliff at dawn is the purest.

黎明时分海崖上的第一滴露水最纯净。

**[166]** Give it to your mommy and she will get better.

给妈妈喝下，她就会好起来。

**[167]** Yay. Are you talking to a bird? It says do can help mommy wake up.

耶。你在跟鸟说话？它说露水能帮妈妈醒来。

**[168]** Children really do have wild imaginations.

小孩子想象力就是丰富。

**[169]** Brother, can you help me get the cup? I can't reach it.

哥哥，能帮我拿一下杯子吗？我够不着。

**[170]** [snorts] Brother, you're so great. Since my legs were injured, Coral is the only one who has called me great.

【哼声】哥哥，你真棒。自从腿受伤后，只有珊瑚叫我真棒。

**[171]** Here. This drop is the clearest. Thank you.

给。这一滴最清澈。谢谢。

**[172]** Mommy, please wake up soon. We all miss you.

妈妈，请快点醒来。我们都想你。

**[173]** For one moment, I actually felt I should trust Coral and believe mom would really wake up.

有那么一瞬间，我真觉得该相信珊瑚，相信妈妈真的会醒。

**[174]** [sighs and gasps] Who are you? Mommy, you're finally awake.

【叹息与喘息】你是谁？妈妈，你终于醒了。

**[175]** Are you Coral? Mhm. Janie, you've been unconscious.

你是珊瑚吗？嗯。珍妮，你一直昏迷着。

**[176]** How did you know about Coral? I heard her call me mommy.

你怎么知道珊瑚？我听见她叫我妈妈。

**[177]** I heard her say she was waiting for me to wake up.

我听见她说在等我醒来。

**[178]** When she fed me the dew, it felt like I floated up from the deep sea.

她喂我喝露水时，我感觉自己像从深海里浮了上来。

**[179]** And then I woke up. I told you she's our lucky star.

然后我就醒了。我就说她是我们的小福星。

**[180]** [laughter] You need to finish the design as soon as possible.

【笑声】你得尽快完成设计。
