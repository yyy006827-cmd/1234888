**[226]** [gasps] Sister, you're awake. Mommy and daddy are in danger.

【倒吸一口气】姐姐，你醒了。妈咪和爹地有危险。

**[227]** What? What's wrong? can't come up with any proof, so you're just framing people.

什么？怎么了？拿不出证据，就只会诬陷别人。

**[228]** [snorts] Janie, admit in public that this work is mine and apologize, and I won't pursue your false accusation.

【冷哼】珍妮，当众承认这作品是我的，并向我道歉，我就不追究你的诬告。

**[229]** Lilith is right. Juke Finn, kneel and admit your mistake.

莉莉丝说得对。芬恩公爵，跪下认错。

**[230]** Then we're even. How about it, Finn? I told you I'd repay your humiliation a thousand times over.

这样我们就算两清。怎么样，芬恩？我说过，会把你对我的羞辱加倍奉还。

**[231]** HOW DARE YOU HIT ME? Bark one more time and Black Reef Bay gets a new master today.

你竟敢打我？再吠一声，今天黑礁湾就换主人。

**[232]** Worry about yourself first. [screaming] Stop.

你还是先担心你自己吧。【尖叫】住手。

**[233]** She's not dead. Coral, you're awake. Didn't she fall into the sea and die?

她没死。珊瑚，你醒了。她不是掉进海里死了吗？

**[234]** How is she still alive? Whose work is this?

她怎么还活着？这是谁的作品？

**[235]** Your highness. This is my work. Lilith, this is clearly mine.

殿下。这是我的作品。莉莉丝，这明明是我的。

**[236]** You're a shameless thief. [snorts] You bad woman.

你这个不要脸的小偷。【冷哼】你这个坏女人。

**[237]** You stole my mommy's work and pushed me into the sea.

你偷了我妈咪的作品，还把我推进海里。

**[238]** Do you have proof? I'm not lying. My name is on that drawing.

你有证据吗？我没有说谎。那张图纸上有我的名字。

**[239]** See, this child lies without thinking. Where is your name on it?

看吧，这孩子张口就撒谎。上面哪有你的名字？

**[240]** Her parents taught her this. Duke Finn's family is a disgrace to the kingdom.

都是她父母教的。芬恩公爵一家真是王国的耻辱。

**[241]** Daddy. Seagull said words written with her feather only appear after touching seawater.

爹地。海鸥说，用它羽毛写的字，只有碰到海水才会显现。

**[242]** Okay. Daddy will get you sea water. I can't believe Lilith really is a thief.

好。爹地这就去取海水。真没想到莉莉丝居然是个小偷。

**[243]** I heard she and Janie used to be good friends.

我听说她和珍妮以前是好朋友。

**[244]** How could she do this? This evidence was found by the cliff.

她怎么能做出这种事？这个证据是在悬崖边发现的。

**[245]** I had a craftsman verify it. You bought this earring.

我找工匠鉴定过了。这耳环是你买的。

**[246]** Save me. I DON'T WANT TO GO TO PRISON. VEIL.

救救我。我不要进监狱。薇尔。

**[247]** LILITH. I never knew you were this evil.

莉莉丝。我从没想过你这么恶毒。

**[248]** Let go of me. [clears throat] I announce that the winner of this design competition is Miss Janie.

放开我。【清嗓子】我宣布，本次设计大赛的冠军是珍妮小姐。

**[249]** Welcome to the king's birthday banquet. [screaming] [sighs] It's all thanks to Coral.

欢迎参加国王的寿宴。【尖叫】【叹气】这一切都多亏了珊瑚。

**[250]** Coral will chase away every bad person who bullies mommy and daddy.

珊瑚会赶走每一个欺负妈咪和爹地的坏人。

**[251]** That birth mark is real. Could she really be my daughter?

那个胎记是真的。她真的会是我的女儿吗？

**[252]** Ever since Coral came to White Cliff Harbor, everything has gone Finn's way.

自从珊瑚来到白崖港，凡事都朝着芬恩有利的方向走。

**[253]** Did the Diver get it backward? Is she actually a lucky star?

难道占卜师算反了？她其实是个小福星？

**[254]** Finn, your good days are over. Once I confirm Coral is my daughter, I'll take her back.

芬恩，你的好日子到头了。一旦确认珊瑚是我女儿，我就把她带回去。

**[255]** Congratulations, Duke Finn and Lady Janie.

恭喜芬恩公爵和珍妮夫人。

**[256]** I truly knew nothing about Lilith's actions.

我对莉莉丝的所作所为一无所知。

**[257]** Coral, I apologize for Lilith. Can you forgive her?

珊瑚，我替莉莉丝道歉。你能原谅她吗？

**[258]** Stay away from my daughter. Daddy, I don't want to see him.

离我女儿远点。爹地，我不想见他。

**[259]** Can we go eat little cakes? Okay, let's go.

我们去吃小蛋糕好不好？好，走吧。

**[260]** The birthmark, the hair, and the birth night records all match.

胎记、头发，还有出生那晚的记录全都对得上。

**[261]** She is that child from back then. She really is my daughter.

她就是当年那个孩子。她真的是我的女儿。

**[262]** I'll claim her at the king's birthday banquet and take back everything that should be mine.

我会在国王的寿宴上认亲，把本该属于我的一切都夺回来。

**[263]** Thank you, Lucky Star, for helping mommy win the competition.

谢谢你，小福星，帮妈咪赢了比赛。

**[264]** Thank you, Grandpa. Coral, watch out. Mark, your legs.

谢谢爷爷。珊瑚，小心。看你的腿。

**[265]** I can stand. [gasps] Coral, you truly are our little lucky star.

我能站起来了。【倒吸一口气】珊瑚，你真是我们的小福星。

**[266]** We're so lucky to be your family. [laughter] Finn, you won't be proud for long.

能成为你的家人，我们真幸运。【笑声】芬恩，你得意不了多久了。

**[267]** Coral will be mine soon. I think I'm lost.

珊瑚很快就是我的了。我觉得我迷路了。

**[268]** Little dog, why aren't you moving? My tummy hurts from eating too much.

小狗狗，你怎么不动了？我肚子好痛，吃太多了。

**[269]** Don't touch my Lumi. The late queen bought it for me.

别碰我的露米。是先皇后买给我的。

**[270]** It has been with me for 10 years. How could it suddenly be gone?

它陪了我十年。怎么会突然没了？
