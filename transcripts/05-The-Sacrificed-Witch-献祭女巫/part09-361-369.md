**[361]** She is your wife! Wife? For the glory of this house I will sacrifice anything and anyone!

她是你的妻子！妻子？为了家族的荣光，我可以牺牲任何事、任何人！

**[362]** Call the demon! Ella run! Don't worry about me He's lost his humanity!

叫那恶魔出来！艾拉，快跑！别管我！他已经丧失人性了！

**[363]** Damn it after giving up half my power You never saw me as family To you Iwas just a pawn 8 An object to be used and thrown away on a whim You dare strike me?

该死，失去一半力量之后……你从未把我当家人。在你眼里，我只是棋子，一件随时可用、随时可弃的工具。你竟敢打我？

**[364]** I am the Duke! I am your father!

我是公爵！我是你父亲！

**[365]** You are no father of mine My father wouldn't lock me in a cell throw me onto an altar or hold a blade to my mother's throat Find a doctor Someone find a doctor I won't kill you But every single one of your crimes sacrificing innocent women colluding with dark entities slaughtering commoners for blood I will make everything public You will lose your wealth your title and your dignity You will be cast out despised by the world and left to rot in utter humiliation No you can't I am the Duke MIN Lucien?

你根本不是我的父亲。我的父亲不会把我关进牢房、把我扔上祭坛，也不会把刀架在我母亲的喉咙上。找医生！快找医生！我不会杀你。但你犯下的每一桩罪——献祭无辜女子、勾结黑暗势力、屠戮平民取血——我都会公之于众。你会失去财富、爵位与尊严，被世人唾弃，在屈辱中腐烂。不，你不能——我是公爵！卢西恩？

**[366]** Looking for me?

在找我？

**[367]** I was so terrified Maurice said there was a chance you'd never wake up I had a nightmare that you dissolved into nothing trying to save me I'm not that stupid Good No matter what happens promise me you'll take care of yourself first Just be gentle - My Lady Witch are you satisfied with my performance?

我害怕极了。莫里斯说你有可能永远醒不过来。我梦见你为救我消散成虚无。我没那么蠢。很好。无论发生什么，答应我先照顾好自己。轻一点——女巫小姐，对我的表现满意吗？

**[368]** Oh so you know how to hide your claws now?

哦，现在倒学会收起爪子了？

**[369]** Back in the bedchamber you didn't show an ounce of gentlemanly restraint That's because i wanted to belong completely to you my beautiful witch Sounds like you have zero regrets My arrogant little demon perhaps i should remind you who sets the rules around here Please punish me

在卧房里，你可一点绅士克制都没有。那是因为我想彻底属于你，我美丽的女巫。听起来你毫无悔意。我傲慢的小恶魔，或许该提醒你，这里谁说了算。请惩罚我吧。
